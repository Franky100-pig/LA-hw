Linear Algebra – Problem Set 1

Determinants, Cofactors & Adjugate, Cramer's Rule · Full solutions · Original problems in the spirit of MIT 18.06 (Strang)

Problem 1

$A$ and $B$ are $4\times 4$ matrices with $\det A = 2$ and $\det B = -3$. Compute each of the following without finding any entries of $A$ or $B$: (a) $\det(AB)$ (b) $\det(A^T)$ (c) $\det(A^{-1}B)$ (d) $\det(2A)$ (e) $\det(A^2B^{-1})$.

Problem 2

$A$ is a $3\times 3$ matrix with $\det A = -4$. A matrix $B$ is obtained from $A$ by performing, in order: $R_2 \leftarrow 3R_2$, then $R_1 \leftrightarrow R_2$, then $R_3 \leftarrow R_3 - 5R_1$. Find $\det B$ and explain the effect of each operation.

Problem 3

Starting from a $3\times 3$ matrix $A$, the operations $R_2 \leftarrow 2R_2$, then $R_1 \leftrightarrow R_3$, then $R_3 \leftarrow R_3 + 4R_2$ are performed, producing $$U = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & -1 & 5 \\ 0 & 0 & 4 \end{bmatrix}.$$ Find $\det A$ without reconstructing $A$.

Problem 4

True or false? Justify each answer (if false, give a counterexample or correct the statement).

Problem 5

Let $A_t = \begin{bmatrix} t & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & t & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & t \end{bmatrix}$. Using determinant properties and row operations (not a full cofactor expansion), find $\det(A_t)$. For which values of $t$ is $A_t$ invertible?

Problem 6

Compute $\det\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 & 2 \\ 2 & 5 & 1 & 4 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 3 & 1 & 3 \end{bmatrix}$ by row-replacement operations only (no swaps), reducing to upper triangular form.

Problem 7

Compute $\det\begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 4 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 5 & 0 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$ by cofactor expansion, choosing the most efficient row or column and stating your choice.

Problem 8

Let $A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1 & 4 \\ 3 & 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 2 & 3 \end{bmatrix}$. Find the cofactors $C_{21}, C_{22}, C_{23}$, then compute $\det A$ by expanding along the second row. Explain the difference between a minor $M_{ij}$ and a cofactor $C_{ij}$.

Problem 9

Let $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 2 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$. (a) Find the cofactor matrix $C$ and $\operatorname{adj}(A)$. (b) Verify one complete row-by-column product in $A\,\operatorname{adj}(A)$. (c) Find $A^{-1}$.

Problem 10

Use Cramer's Rule to solve $\begin{cases} 3x - 2y = 4 \\ 2x + 5y = 9 \end{cases}$. Identify $A$, $A_1$, $A_2$ and compute the necessary determinants.

Problem 11

Consider the system $$\begin{cases} x + 2y + z = 5 \\ 2x - y + 3z = 4 \\ x + y + 2z = 7 \end{cases}$$ Use Cramer's Rule to find only $y$. Do not solve for $x$ or $z$.

Problem 12

(a) Suppose every row of a square matrix $A$ sums to zero. Prove that $\det A = 0$ without expanding the determinant.  (b) True or false: "$\operatorname{adj}(A)$ exists only when $A$ is invertible." Justify your answer.
